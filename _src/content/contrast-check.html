> CONTRAST_CHECK

Foreground/background pair → the actual WCAG 2.x contrast ratio, checked against all four real thresholds (AA/AAA, normal/large text). If a pair fails AA for normal text, this searches for the closest lightness adjustment to either color that would pass, rather than just telling you it failed and leaving you to guess.
