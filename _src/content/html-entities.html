> HTML_CODEC

Direction is auto-detected - paste an entity string to decode it, or plain text to escape it. Escaping covers the 5 reserved HTML characters plus any non-ASCII codepoint as a numeric entity. Decoding runs through DOMParser against a detached document that's never attached to the live page, rather than the classic innerHTML-on-a-textarea trick - that older approach can be broken out of with a literal </textarea> in the input, letting whatever comes after it render as real markup instead of decoded text.
