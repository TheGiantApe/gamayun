> WHAT A PHOTO CARRIES WITHOUT YOU KNOWING

Every photo out of a modern camera or phone carries hidden EXIF data - camera model, exact timestamp, and often precise GPS coordinates - unless it's been stripped. Reverse image search tools can also tell you where else online a given image has appeared, which is how a lot of "this photo is old, it's not actually from the event people are claiming" debunks get done.

Full breakdown of how reverse image search actually works, and the specific EXIF fields worth checking before you post anything, lives in the GAMA+ Wiki.
