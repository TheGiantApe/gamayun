> PASSWORD_GEN

Uses crypto.getRandomValues(), a real cryptographic random source, not Math.random() (which was never designed to be unpredictable and shouldn't back anything security-relevant). It's also rejection-sampled: picking a character via a plain modulo on a random byte slightly favors whichever characters land at the low end of the byte range whenever the character set size doesn't divide 256 evenly - a bias most generators never bother correcting, small per character but compounding across a whole password. Shows a real entropy estimate below the result, not just a length number.
