> WHY THE RANDOMNESS SOURCE ACTUALLY MATTERS HERE

                
Every mode above pulls from crypto.getRandomValues(), not Math.random() - the same CSPRNG standard as this site's security tools. It doesn't change how a coin flip feels, but it's the difference between "random enough for a board game" and "actually unpredictable," and there's no real cost to just using the better source by default.
