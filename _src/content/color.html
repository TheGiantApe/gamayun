> COLOR_CONVERTER

Paste any color in any of four formats - hex, RGB integer (0-255), RGB float (0-1, the range shaders/OpenGL/Unity actually expect instead of 0-255), or HSL - and get all four back at once. The float format specifically is the one most color-picker tools skip entirely, which means a real gap for anyone working in a graphics pipeline that doesn't speak 0-255.
