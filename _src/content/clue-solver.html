> CLUE SOLVER

Real constraint propagation, not just a marked-up grid: mark a card YES or NO for any player or the Case File and the solver fills in everything it can prove from that - a card confirmed for one owner clears it everywhere else, a card cleared for every player locks it into the Case File, and a category down to one remaining candidate gets solved automatically. Auto-deduced cells are locked (dimmed border) so a stray click can't fight the logic - click a manual cell to cycle unknown → yes → no. Independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Hasbro or the Clue/Cluedo board game.
