> UUID_GEN

v4 UUIDs from a real cryptographic random source, not Math.random().


            

            
See also: Hash Generator


            

                
> WHY THESE DON'T COLLIDE

                
A version-4 UUID has 122 genuinely random bits. Birthday-paradox math says you'd need roughly 2.71 quintillion of them before the odds of any two colliding crossed 50% - a number large enough that it's not really a probability question anymore, it's a "the universe will have other problems first" question.

                
Full breakdown, including why other UUID versions exist at all and when you'd actually want one of them instead, lives in the GAMA+ Wiki.
