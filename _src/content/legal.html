Privacy Policy

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Files, URLs, and text you enter into a tool are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to, or stored on, any server we control. We have no accounts, no login, and nothing to breach on that front because there's nothing to steal. That part hasn't changed and isn't going to.

As of 2026-07, this site runs Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Here's exactly what that does and doesn't mean: we don't collect your name, email, or any personal information, and we've never sold or shared user data — that's not the model here and never will be. What Analytics gives us is aggregate, anonymized usage patterns (which tools get used, how often, roughly how) so we know what's actually worth building more of instead of guessing blind. That's the only reason it's there. Nothing you type or upload into a specific tool is ever part of that data — see above, it never leaves your browser regardless.

Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, page requested) may be retained briefly by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention, per their own policy. Analytics and ads on this site set their own cookies per Google's privacy policy, which we don't control, only enable — see Google's privacy policy for the full detail, or use a browser privacy extension if you'd rather opt out; nothing on this site requires analytics or ads to function.

Short version: we're not in the data business. We're in the make-useful-tools-and-don't-be-gross-about-it business.
