> TOS_SCAN

Paste any Terms of Service, EULA, or contract text. Flags common problematic clause patterns (data sale, forced arbitration, auto-renewal, broad content licenses, and more) with a plain-English explanation of each. Rule-based pattern matching, not legal advice and not a substitute for actually reading the thing - a first pass, not a verdict.

> WHY NOBODY READS THESE

The average Terms of Service document is written to be technically disclosed and practically unread - long, dense, and rarely where the terms that actually matter to you (data sale, arbitration, auto-renewal) are easy to find. A project called Terms of Service; Didn't Read exists specifically to grade services on what their terms actually say, in plain language, rather than what the marketing page implies.

Full breakdown of clause types, notable ToS controversies, and why "I agree" mostly isn't optional anymore lives in the GAMA+ Wiki.
