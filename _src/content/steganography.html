> STEGANOGRAPHY

Hide a text message inside an image's pixel data (LSB encoding - one bit per color channel, invisible to the eye). Output is always PNG: JPEG's lossy recompression destroys the hidden bits, so re-saving or re-uploading a stego image anywhere that re-encodes it as JPEG will wipe the message. Nothing is uploaded anywhere - the image never leaves your browser.
