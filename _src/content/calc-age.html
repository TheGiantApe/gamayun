> AGE_CALCULATOR

Exact age as a real calendar breakdown - years, months, and days - not just a division of total days by 365.25. The two disagree more often than you'd expect (leap years, and months of different lengths, both throw off a naive day-count average), so this does the actual calendar-aware subtraction, borrowing a month or year the same way you'd work it out by hand. The raw total day count is shown alongside it too, for whichever framing you actually need.
