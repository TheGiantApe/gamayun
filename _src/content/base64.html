> BASE64_CODEC

Base64 is the text-safe encoding behind embedding images in CSS/HTML, packing binary data into JSON or a URL, and most "data:" URIs. This one handles UTF-8 correctly - a plain btoa()/atob() call (still common in quick online tools) breaks the moment the input has anything outside plain ASCII, silently mangling emoji, accented letters, or non-Latin text instead of erroring honestly. Paste either a Base64 string or plain text; the toggle below picks the direction.
