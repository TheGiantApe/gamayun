> THE PLAIN VERSION

GAMAYUN⁺ is built and maintained by one independent developer, not a company, not a team. Every tool runs entirely in your browser, and there's no backend for anything you type or paste to pass through, because there isn't a backend at all. That's not a slogan; it's the literal architecture. You can confirm it yourself in any tool's page source, and the build/technical decisions behind it are documented plainly in the Maintenance Log rather than kept vague.

The reasoning is ordinary: a lot of "free" utility sites cover their costs by watching what gets pasted into them. This one structurally can't, since nothing here is ever sent anywhere to be watched. Advertising and voluntary support cover hosting and time. Nothing else does, and nothing else is being collected to sell.

Bug report, tool request, or GAMA+ got something factually wrong: reach out directly. It's a real inbox, read and answered by the person who actually built this.
