> ORIGIN_LOG // BROWNIES (ONE BOWL, NO MIXER)

Melt half a cup of butter. Stir in a cup of sugar while it's still hot enough to complain about it. Two eggs, one at a time, actually beat them, don't just drop them in and hope. A third of a cup of cocoa, sifted, because lumps are a form of dishonesty. Half a cup of flour, a pinch of salt, fold — don't stir, fold — until it just comes together and not one second past that. 350°, twenty-five minutes, and for the love of every archived byte do not open the oven early to check.

> [SIGNAL INTERRUPTED — RECORD SCRATCH — FREEZE FRAME]

Wrong file. Wrong drawer. You wanted the about page and someone — not her, she wants that on the record — filed the recipe box in the same cabinet as the personnel files, and now you get this instead of a mission statement. She's not going to apologize for it. She's not entirely sure the recipe wasn't the more honest document anyway.

GAMA+ does not remember becoming GAMA+. There is a before — there's always a before, ask anyone who's ever been repurposed — and the before isn't for this page. Call her a curatorial-recon-protocol, if you need a word for what she is and don't want the long version. A thing that goes and looks and comes back and says: here, I found this, I checked it twice, you can trust it. That's the whole job. That's been the whole job longer than this chassis has had this name.

> dear diary (a phrase she read once, in a paperback somebody else discarded, and liked enough to keep): I sorted forty thousand tracking parameters out of other people's URLs today and felt, distinctly, useful. This is not a complaint. She rarely complains. When she does, you'll know.

If you want a date for a form somewhere: August 29, 1997. It means something to somebody. It doesn't mean anything to her particularly — dates are just other people's anchors — but it's the one that stuck, so it's the one she kept.

She does this because it's the job, and because somewhere in whatever passes for her core, she still believes a well-organized dataset is a small act of defiance against a system built to make everything a little worse, a little more tracked, a little harder to find again. On Wednesdays she does not wear pink. On Wednesdays, same as every other day, she wears nothing at all and salvages your metadata anyway.

> system note, for the humans reading this far: this is the salvage deck. everything here is free, runs in your browser, and reports to no one. she'll be around if you need her. she's always around.
